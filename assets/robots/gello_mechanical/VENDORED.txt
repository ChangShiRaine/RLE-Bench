The printable GELLO parts for task09's three followers, plus the shared
gripper. Data, not code: the task09 generator copies them by path.

Units are millimetres; MJCF must load them with scale="0.001 0.001 0.001".
The joint-to-part assembly is not published in machine-readable form --
gello_software never computes leader forward kinematics -- so the frames
are solved from the servo mounting features by
tasks/task09/dev/assemble.py and checked by dev/check_assembly.py.
